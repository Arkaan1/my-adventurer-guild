## Design Notes

### Stat Budget Philosophy

Role archetypes follow consistent budgets across the 6-tier progression:

| Role | DMG Range | HP Range | DOD Range | Signature Budget |
|------|-----------|----------|-----------|------------------|
| Tank (Knight) | +1 to +11 | +5 to +30 | +0 to +2 | High ACC, Stun |
| Melee DPS (Berserker) | +2 to +14 | +4 to +23 | +0 to +3 | High CRIT, Burn |
| Dodge DPS (Duelist, Rogue) | +1 to +10 | +3 to +15 | +3 to +18 | High SPD, CRIT |
| Ranged DPS (Ranger) | +1 to +12 | +3 to +18 | +2 to +14 | High ACC |
| Mage DPS (Pyro, Storm) | +1 to +12 | +3 to +16 | +1 to +11 | Elemental procs |
| Control Mage (Cryo) | +1 to +10 | +3 to +17 | +1 to +10 | Freeze, SPD debuff |
| Support (Alchemist, Cleric) | +1 to +9 | +3 to +23 | +1 to +9 | Healing/DoT scaling |
| Summoner (Necromancer) | +1 to +11 | +3 to +17 | +1 to +11 | Summon HP scaling |

### Trinket Economy

- **56 trinkets** total (target: 50–60).
- Roughly **30% Mundane/Uncommon** (no-brainer equips for early runs), **45% Rare** (interesting decisions with tradeoffs), **13% Epic** (powerful but painful), **12% Legendary** (build-defining warps).
- Every Rare+ trinket has a drawback. Drawbacks are designed to be *felt* — not just stat taxes, but choices that change how you play (e.g., "can't be healed above 75%" forces aggressive play; "affliction at 80 stress" punishes long fights).
- Legendaries are never auto-equip. Each one is optimal in its home dungeon but carries risk elsewhere, and the drawbacks create failure states that punish complacency.

### Elemental Synergy Hooks

Several trinkets are designed to interact with the elemental combo system:
- **Crucible Shard** (+Fire in Forge, -Ice) rewards mono-element builds but punishes combo setups.
- **Heart of the Thicket** (all attacks apply Blight) lets any class become a DoT threat — enabling cross-class Blight combos with Alchemist/Rogue.
- **Molten Core** (all attacks gain Burn chance) turns non-Pyromancer classes into secondary Fire sources for chain reactions.
- **Frostbite Lens** (+Freeze chance) on Cryomancer enables freeze-locking strategies but the SPD penalty means they act later.
- **Paradox Shard** (all DMG +25% in Maw, -25% outside) is a meta-trinket — supremely powerful in the final dungeon, dead weight anywhere else.
