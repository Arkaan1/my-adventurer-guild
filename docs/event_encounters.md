### 1. The Fallen Party

**Category:** Generic | **Rarity:** Common

**Narrative setup:**
Four corpses slumped against the tunnel wall, still in marching order. Their armor is dented, their faces frozen in expressions ranging from terror to resignation. One clutches a leather satchel to their chest even in death. Flies haven't found them yet — they died recently. Whatever killed them left no visible wound.

**Choices:**

**A) "Search the bodies."**
- Requirements: None
- Outcome: The party loots the dead. Simple, practical, grim.
- Mechanical effects: Gain 150-300 gold (random). 50% chance to find 1 random supply item. +10 stress to all heroes (desecration guilt).

**B) "Take only the satchel."**
- Requirements: None
- Outcome: The satchel contains a journal and a single item. The journal's final entry describes something stalking them — something that made no sound.
- Mechanical effects: Gain 1 random trinket (common or uncommon). +5 stress to all heroes. Grants scouting for the next 3 rooms (the journal has a partial map).

**C) "Bury them and move on."**
- Requirements: None
- Outcome: The party spends precious time digging shallow graves. It feels decent. It feels foolish.
- Mechanical effects: Lose 1 Shovel (if available; if not, -5 HP to 2 random heroes from digging with hands). -15 stress to all heroes. No loot.

**D) "The Necromancer communes with the dead."**
- Requirements: Necromancer in party
- Outcome: The Necromancer touches the nearest corpse's forehead and listens. The dead man's final moments flood in — along with his knowledge of the dungeon ahead.
- Mechanical effects: Full scouting of current floor. +15 stress to Necromancer. 25% chance Necromancer gains quirk: **"Voices of the Dead"** (positive: +10% scouting chance; negative: +5 stress when camping).

**Stat-dependent modifier:** If any hero has stress > 120, they refuse to approach the bodies — that hero cannot participate in choice A and gains +10 stress regardless of choice made.

### 2. The Desperate Stranger

**Category:** Generic | **Rarity:** Common

**Narrative setup:**
A figure limps from the shadows, one arm hanging useless, eyes wild with pain. They wear the tattered remains of a guild tabard — they were part of a sanctioned expedition. "Please," they rasp. "Bandages. Anything. I can pay — I found something down here. Something valuable." They hold up a cloth-wrapped object with their good hand.

**Choices:**

**A) "Help them. Use your supplies."**
- Requirements: 1 Bandage
- Outcome: The stranger weeps with relief. They press the wrapped object into your hands and limp toward the exit. Whether they make it is not your problem.
- Mechanical effects: Lose 1 Bandage. Gain 1 random trinket (uncommon-rare weighted 70/30). -10 stress to all heroes.

**B) "Take the object. Leave them."**
- Requirements: None
- Outcome: The stranger doesn't resist — they don't have the strength. They simply stare as you take the bundle and walk past. One hero glances back. The rest don't.
- Mechanical effects: Gain 1 random trinket (common-uncommon weighted 50/50). +15 stress to all heroes. 20% chance a random hero gains quirk: **"Cold-Blooded"** (positive: +5% stress resist; negative: -10% healing received).

**C) "Demand information instead."**
- Requirements: None
- Outcome: You refuse the object and instead press the stranger for intelligence. They're delirious, but through the babbling, useful details emerge — patrol routes, trap locations, a hidden cache.
- Mechanical effects: Scouting for the next 5 rooms. Gain 200 gold (the hidden cache). Stranger dies behind you — +10 stress to rear two heroes.

**D) "Walk away."**
- Requirements: None
- Outcome: Not your problem. You leave them in the dark.
- Mechanical effects: No cost, no reward. +5 stress to all heroes (the sound of quiet sobbing follows you for two corridors).

### 3. The Cursed Reliquary

**Category:** Generic | **Rarity:** Uncommon

**Narrative setup:**
A stone chest sits on a plinth, covered in ward-glyphs that have been scratched through — deliberately broken. The air around it hums. Inside, visible through a crack in the lid, something pulses with violet light. The wards were placed to keep whatever is inside contained. Someone wanted them removed.

**Choices:**

**A) "Open the reliquary."**
- Requirements: None
- Outcome (Gamble):
  - 40% — The item inside is a powerful trinket, and the curse has faded. Gain 1 rare trinket. +5 Arcane Swelling.
  - 35% — The curse lashes out. 1 random hero takes 25% max HP damage and gains debuff: **"Hexed"** (-15% damage dealt for rest of mission). +10 Arcane Swelling.
  - 25% — The item crumbles to dust the moment air touches it. +15 stress to all heroes. +5 Arcane Swelling. Nothing gained.

**B) "Reseal the wards."**
- Requirements: None
- Outcome: The party spends time re-carving the protective glyphs. It's painstaking work and costs the party dearly in focus, but the danger is contained.
- Mechanical effects: -10 Arcane Swelling (global). +10 stress to all heroes (the concentration is exhausting). No loot.

**C) "The Alchemist analyzes the resonance."**
- Requirements: Alchemist in party
- Outcome: The Alchemist studies the pulse pattern and identifies the curse type before opening. They can extract the item safely — mostly.
- Mechanical effects: Gain 1 rare trinket. +10 stress to Alchemist. +5 Arcane Swelling. Alchemist gains buff: **"Arcane Insight"** (+10% debuff skill chance for rest of mission).

### 4. The Sealed Door

**Category:** Generic | **Rarity:** Common

**Narrative setup:**
A heavy iron door blocks the corridor, bolted from this side — whoever sealed it wanted to keep something in, not out. Scratches cover the inner surface. Deep, rhythmic thuds emanate from beyond. Whatever is behind it is still alive, and it knows you're here.

**Choices:**

**A) "Open it."**
- Requirements: None
- Outcome (Gamble):
  - 50% — A trapped creature, weakened from captivity. Easy combat encounter (reduced enemy HP). Room beyond contains 300-500 gold and 1 supply item.
  - 50% — The creature is not weakened. Standard combat encounter at +25% enemy damage buff. Same loot on victory.

**B) "Barricade it further and leave."**
- Requirements: None
- Outcome: You reinforce the door. Whatever is behind it roars and slams harder. The door holds. Probably.
- Mechanical effects: No reward. +5 stress to all heroes. Safe.

**C) "The Rogue picks the lock quietly."**
- Requirements: Rogue in party
- Outcome: The Rogue opens the door silently, granting the party a surprise round. Combat encounter, but the party gets a full free round of actions before the enemy responds.
- Mechanical effects: Combat with surprise round advantage. Same loot as choice A. +5 stress to Rogue (being closest to the door when it opens).

**Stat-dependent modifier:** If any hero has Dodge > 40, they can volunteer to open the door and leap back — the creature's first attack auto-misses that hero.

### 5. The Whispering Idol

**Category:** Generic | **Rarity:** Uncommon

**Narrative setup:**
Carved from black stone that drinks the torchlight, the idol depicts a figure with too many arms and no face. It whispers — not in words, but in impressions. Hunger. Offering. Reward. Every hero feels it differently: the Knight feels it as duty, the Rogue as opportunity, the Cleric as blasphemy. It wants something from you. It will give something in return.

**Choices:**

**A) "Offer gold."**
- Requirements: 300+ gold
- Outcome: The gold dissolves into the stone. The whisper becomes a purr. Each hero feels a warmth spread through their muscles.
- Mechanical effects: Lose 300 gold. All heroes gain buff: **"Idol's Favor"** (+10% damage, +5% crit for rest of mission). +5 Arcane Swelling.

**B) "Offer blood."**
- Requirements: None
- Outcome: A hero steps forward and cuts their palm against the idol's outstretched hand. The stone drinks deeply.
- Mechanical effects: 1 hero (player-chosen) loses 30% current HP. That hero gains buff: **"Blood Pact"** (+25% damage, +15% crit for rest of mission) and debuff: **"Bleeding Covenant"** (cannot be healed above 70% max HP for rest of mission). +10 Arcane Swelling.

**C) "Smash the idol."**
- Requirements: None
- Outcome: The first blow cracks it. The second shatters it. The whisper becomes a shriek that rakes across every mind, then silence. Something watches you now from further away.
- Mechanical effects: +25 stress to all heroes. -10 Arcane Swelling. 1 random hero gains quirk: **"Iconoclast"** (positive: +15% stress resist vs. eldritch enemies; negative: cannot interact with shrines).

**D) "Pray before it."**
- Requirements: Cleric in party
- Outcome: The Cleric kneels not in worship but in opposition — channeling divine light against the idol's darkness. It's a contest of faith. The idol trembles.
- Mechanical effects: -15 Arcane Swelling. Cleric loses 20% max HP (the effort is immense). Cleric gains buff: **"Sanctified"** (+20% healing power for rest of mission). All other heroes: -15 stress.

### 6. The Trapped Corridor

**Category:** Generic | **Rarity:** Common

**Narrative setup:**
The floor tiles ahead are uneven — deliberately so. Pressure plates. The walls have narrow slots at ankle and neck height. On the far side, a supply cache sits in plain view, practically begging someone to walk across. This is not subtle. Whoever built this wanted you to see the reward and weigh it against the danger.

**Choices:**

**A) "Rush through."**
- Requirements: None
- Outcome (Gamble): Each hero individually runs the gauntlet.
  - Per hero: 60% chance of making it through unscathed, 40% chance of taking a dart/blade.
  - Hit: -15 HP and 50% chance of debuff **"Poisoned"** (-3 HP per room for 5 rooms).
- Mechanical effects: On reaching the other side, gain 2 random supply items + 200 gold.

**B) "Send one hero through carefully."**
- Requirements: None
- Outcome: One hero inches through, testing each tile. Slower, more careful, but they're alone if something goes wrong.
- Per hero sent: 80% safe, 20% hit for -25 HP (the traps hit harder when you're moving slow — you can't dodge).
- Mechanical effects: Gain 2 random supply items + 200 gold.

**C) "Disarm the traps."**
- Requirements: Rogue in party OR 1 Trap Disarm Kit supply
- Outcome: The Rogue's fingers move with practiced precision, or the kit provides the tools. Either way, the corridor is neutralized.
- Mechanical effects: Safe passage. Gain 2 random supply items + 200 gold. +5 stress to Rogue (one trigger was a hair's breadth from firing). If using kit: lose 1 Trap Disarm Kit.

**D) "Leave it."**
- Requirements: None
- Outcome: The cache sits there, mocking your caution.
- Mechanical effects: No risk, no reward. +5 stress to all heroes (cowardice nags).

**Stat-dependent modifier:** Heroes with Dodge > 35 have their hit chance reduced to 20% in choice A.

### 7. The Merchant of Depths

**Category:** Generic | **Rarity:** Uncommon

**Narrative setup:**
A hunched figure sits behind a moth-eaten blanket spread with wares — trinkets, potions, bandages, and one item that seems to shimmer differently from the rest. The merchant grins with too many teeth. "Everything has a price, dear traveler. And everything is for sale. Even things you didn't know you were selling." Their prices are steep. Their stock is genuine. How they survive down here is a question best left unasked.

**Choices:**

**A) "Buy supplies."**
- Requirements: 200+ gold
- Outcome: Standard transaction. The merchant's stock is overpriced but authentic.
- Mechanical effects: Lose 200 gold. Gain 3 supply items of your choice (from: Bandage, Antivenom, Torch, Food, Holy Water).

**B) "Buy the shimmering item."**
- Requirements: 500+ gold
- Outcome: The merchant wraps it carefully, almost reverently. "You won't regret this. Well. You might."
- Mechanical effects: Lose 500 gold. Gain 1 rare trinket (weighted toward powerful items). +5 Arcane Swelling.

**C) "Rob them."**
- Requirements: None
- Outcome: The merchant is not defenseless. Combat encounter — **The Merchant** (high dodge, moderate HP, casts debilitating curses). If victorious:
- Mechanical effects: Gain all items from choices A and B. +25 stress to all heroes. The merchant, dying, curses the party: 1 random hero gains quirk: **"Merchant's Grudge"** (negative: all shop prices +20% permanently).

**D) "Trade a trinket."**
- Requirements: At least 1 trinket in inventory
- Outcome: The merchant evaluates your offering with an appraiser's eye. "Acceptable."
- Mechanical effects: Lose 1 trinket (player-chosen). Gain 1 trinket of equal or higher rarity (random). 30% chance the new trinket is one rarity tier higher. No gold cost.

### 8. The Blood Fountain

**Category:** Generic | **Rarity:** Rare

**Narrative setup:**
Water does not flow in this chamber — something thicker does. A stone basin shaped like cupped hands overflows with dark crimson liquid that steams faintly in the cold air. It smells of iron and something older. Carved around the basin's rim in an ancient script: "Drink, and know thyself. Drink deep, and lose thyself." One hero's reflection in the liquid looks different — younger, stronger, unmarked.

**Choices:**

**A) "Drink sparingly."**
- Requirements: None
- Outcome (Gamble):
  - 50% — Warmth floods the drinker. Vigor returns. Gain +25% max HP (permanent for this hero, this mission only) and -30 stress.
  - 30% — The blood rejects them. Violent retching. -20 HP and +20 stress. Debuff: **"Tainted Blood"** (-10% max HP for rest of mission).
  - 20% — Visions. The hero sees their own death, somewhere ahead in this dungeon. +40 stress. Gain quirk: **"Death-Marked"** (negative: -10% deathblow resist; positive: +15% damage when below 25% HP).

**B) "Drink deeply."**
- Requirements: None
- Outcome (Gamble):
  - 30% — Transformation. The hero is fundamentally empowered. Permanent quirk: **"Blood-Blessed"** (+15% max HP, +10% damage, -10% stress resist). Full HP restore.
  - 40% — The fountain's hunger awakens. -50% current HP. +50 stress. Debuff: **"Hemorrhaging"** (-5 HP per room, rest of mission). The hero gains an unsettling reddish tint to their eyes.
  - 30% — The hero loses consciousness and must be dragged onward. -75% current HP. +60 stress. Gain quirk: **"Sanguine Addiction"** (negative: the hero demands to interact with any future Blood Fountains; cannot choose "Leave").

**C) "Fill a vial."**
- Requirements: None
- Outcome: You take some for later analysis or use. The fountain's surface ripples — annoyed.
- Mechanical effects: Gain supply item: **"Vial of Crimson"** (single use: fully heal 1 hero, but +30 stress to that hero). +5 Arcane Swelling.

**D) "Shatter the basin."**
- Requirements: None
- Outcome: The liquid hisses as it hits the stone floor and evaporates. The carved script glows briefly, then cracks. Something has been permanently destroyed.
- Mechanical effects: -15 Arcane Swelling. +10 stress to all heroes. 50% chance to find a trinket hidden beneath the basin (uncommon rarity).

### 9. The Crossroads Specter

**Category:** Generic | **Rarity:** Uncommon

**Narrative setup:**
At a junction of three corridors, a translucent figure hovers — the ghost of a cartographer, still clutching their instruments. They died here, mapping. Now they offer to continue their work, for a price. "I remember every passage," the specter murmurs. "I remember every trap, every den, every dead end. I remember being alive, too. That's the part that hurts."

**Choices:**

**A) "Accept the specter's guidance."**
- Requirements: None
- Outcome: The specter drifts ahead, marking your map. Its presence is deeply unsettling but undeniably useful.
- Mechanical effects: Full floor scouting. +20 stress to all heroes (lingering spectral presence). +5 Arcane Swelling.

**B) "Offer the specter peace."**
- Requirements: Holy Water (1)
- Outcome: The Cleric or any hero sprinkles holy water. The specter sighs, smiles faintly, and dissolves. As it fades, it whispers the location of one thing.
- Mechanical effects: Lose 1 Holy Water. -15 stress to all heroes. Reveals the location of the treasure room on this floor (if one exists).

**C) "Bind the specter."**
- Requirements: Necromancer in party
- Outcome: The Necromancer weaves a binding. The specter screams, then complies. It serves as an unwilling scout for the remainder of the floor.
- Mechanical effects: Full floor scouting + 25% chance to auto-detect traps in each room. +15 stress to Necromancer. +10 Arcane Swelling. Necromancer gains debuff: **"Haunted"** (+10 stress per combat for rest of mission — the specter whispers distractions).

**D) "Ignore it."**
- Requirements: None
- Outcome: You walk past. The specter watches you go, fading slowly.
- Mechanical effects: No effect. No cost.

### 10. The Collapsed Shrine

**Category:** Generic | **Rarity:** Common

**Narrative setup:**
What was once a small devotional alcove has been crushed under fallen stone. Through the rubble, a faint golden glow persists — the shrine's divine ember still burns. Reaching it would require moving dangerous amounts of unstable rock. The air tastes of dust and old faith.

**Choices:**

**A) "Dig through the rubble."**
- Requirements: 1 Shovel (optional, improves odds)
- Outcome without Shovel: Difficult manual excavation.
  - 60% success: Reach the ember. All heroes healed 20% max HP. -20 stress to all.
  - 40% failure: Collapse. 2 random heroes take 20 HP damage. No reward.
- Outcome with Shovel: Lose 1 Shovel. 90% success with same rewards. 10% failure: 1 random hero takes 10 HP damage.

**B) "Pray from outside."**
- Requirements: None
- Outcome: You kneel in the dust and rubble. The ember's glow flickers — it hears you, barely.
- Mechanical effects: -10 stress to all heroes. +5 HP to all heroes. Safe but minor.

**C) "The Cleric channels the ember remotely."**
- Requirements: Cleric in party
- Outcome: The Cleric extends their faith through the rubble. Connection is made. The ember blazes bright, then extinguishes — all its power drawn out in one burst.
- Mechanical effects: All heroes healed 30% max HP. -25 stress to all heroes. Cleric loses 10% max HP (strain of channeling). Cleric gains buff: **"Ember-Touched"** (+15% healing skills, rest of mission).

### 11. The Deserter's Cache

**Category:** Generic | **Rarity:** Common

**Narrative setup:**
Behind a loose stone in the wall, someone hid supplies — a deserter's stash for a retreat that never came. The supplies are old but usable. A note reads: "If I don't come back for this, it means I didn't make it. Take what you need. Don't linger." The handwriting gets shakier toward the end.

**Choices:**

**A) "Take everything."**
- Requirements: None
- Outcome: Practical. You clean out the cache.
- Mechanical effects: Gain 3 random supply items + 100 gold. +5 stress to all heroes (a small guilt).

**B) "Take only what you need."**
- Requirements: None
- Outcome: You take conservatively, leaving the rest in case someone else passes through.
- Mechanical effects: Gain 1 supply item of your choice. -5 stress to all heroes.

**C) "Search the area for the deserter."**
- Requirements: None
- Outcome (Gamble):
  - 40% — Find the deserter's body. They didn't make it. Gain their equipment: 1 common trinket + 150 gold + the cache supplies (3 random items).
  - 30% — Find nothing. Wasted time. +10 stress to all heroes (searching in the dark is nerve-wracking).
  - 30% — Find the deserter, alive but broken. They've been surviving in a crevice, half-mad. They beg to join you to the exit. Gain a temporary ally: **"The Deserter"** (low stats, but absorbs one hit that would otherwise strike a hero, then flees). +15 stress to all heroes. Cache supplies gained.

### 12. The Sacrificial Altar

**Category:** Generic | **Rarity:** Uncommon

**Narrative setup:**
An obsidian slab dominates the chamber, its surface channeled with grooves that lead to a central basin. The stone remembers — you can almost see the ghosts of the rituals performed here. Dried blood fills the grooves like a road map of suffering. Above the altar, the air shimmers, and you realize: the altar still functions. It still accepts. It still rewards.

**Choices:**

**A) "Sacrifice gold."**
- Requirements: 400+ gold
- Outcome: The gold dissolves into the basin. Runes illuminate along the channels.
- Mechanical effects: Lose 400 gold. All heroes gain buff: **"Dark Aegis"** (+15% PROT, +10% stress resist for rest of mission). +10 Arcane Swelling.

**B) "Sacrifice a trinket."**
- Requirements: At least 1 trinket
- Outcome: The trinket shatters as it touches the stone. The altar hums with satisfaction.
- Mechanical effects: Lose 1 trinket (player-chosen). 1 hero (player-chosen) gains permanent quirk: **"Altar-Blessed"** (+10% damage, +5% crit). +10 Arcane Swelling.

**C) "Blood sacrifice."**
- Requirements: None
- Outcome: A hero opens their veins over the basin. The altar drinks greedily.
- Mechanical effects: 1 hero (player-chosen) loses 40% current HP. That hero gains buff: **"Exalted Offering"** (+25% damage, +20% crit, +15% speed for rest of mission). +15 Arcane Swelling. 20% chance hero gains quirk: **"Sacrificial Compulsion"** (negative: hero insists on interacting with future altars).

**D) "Desecrate the altar."**
- Requirements: 1 Holy Water
- Outcome: Holy Water sizzles on the obsidian. The runes crack and darken. The shimmer dies.
- Mechanical effects: Lose 1 Holy Water. -20 Arcane Swelling. +15 stress to all heroes (the altar's death-scream echoes). 30% chance: 1 random hero gains quirk: **"Purifier"** (positive: +15% damage vs. undead and eldritch).

### 13. The Mirror of Truths

**Category:** Generic | **Rarity:** Rare

**Narrative setup:**
A freestanding mirror, impossibly intact, shows each hero a different reflection. The Knight sees themselves without armor — vulnerable, small. The Pyromancer sees themselves consumed by their own flames. Each hero sees the version of themselves they fear most. But the mirror also shows truths. Its frame bears an inscription: "Gaze long enough and I will show you what lies ahead. Gaze too long, and I will show you what lies within."

**Choices:**

**A) "Gaze into the mirror briefly."**
- Requirements: None
- Outcome: Each hero sees a flash of the dungeon ahead — useful intelligence wrapped in personal horror.
- Mechanical effects: Scouting for 5 rooms. +15 stress to all heroes.

**B) "Gaze deeply."**
- Requirements: None
- Outcome: One hero volunteers to stare into the mirror until it reveals everything.
- Mechanical effects (for chosen hero):
  - Full floor scouting.
  - +40 stress to chosen hero.
  - 50% chance hero gains quirk: **"Self-Aware"** (positive: +10% all resists; negative: +10% stress from crits received — they've seen what can break them).
  - 50% chance hero gains quirk: **"Shattered Confidence"** (negative: -10% damage; positive: +15% dodge — they fight defensively now, always).

**C) "Shatter the mirror."**
- Requirements: None
- Outcome: Seven years of bad luck is a superstition. Down here, it's a promise.
- Mechanical effects: +10 stress to all heroes. -5 Arcane Swelling. 1 random hero gains debuff: **"Mirror-Cursed"** (-5% to all stats for rest of mission). No reward.

**D) "The Stormcaller channels lightning through the frame."**
- Requirements: Stormcaller in party
- Outcome: The electrical surge overloads the mirror's enchantment. Instead of reflections, it projects the entire dungeon floor as a crackling map of light before exploding.
- Mechanical effects: Full floor scouting. +10 stress to all heroes (the explosion). +5 Arcane Swelling. Stormcaller takes 15% max HP damage (feedback). Stormcaller gains buff: **"Overcharged"** (+20% skill damage for rest of mission).

### 14. The Dying Familiar

**Category:** Generic | **Rarity:** Common

**Narrative setup:**
A small creature — a raven with three eyes, feathers singed and one wing broken — lies gasping on the stone floor. It was a wizard's familiar, bound by magic that's now unraveling. It looks at the party with unsettling intelligence. It can't speak, but its eyes communicate clearly: help me, or end it. The binding magic still sparks around it, and that energy could be harnessed.

**Choices:**

**A) "Tend to the creature."**
- Requirements: 1 Bandage
- Outcome: You splint the wing and wrap the wounds. The raven croaks gratefully and perches on the party's pack. It can't fly, but its three eyes see much.
- Mechanical effects: Lose 1 Bandage. Gain buff: **"Familiar's Sight"** (party gains +10% scouting chance, +10% trap detection for rest of mission). -5 stress to all heroes.

**B) "Put it out of its misery."**
- Requirements: None
- Outcome: Quick and merciful. As the creature dies, the binding magic discharges into the nearest hero.
- Mechanical effects: +10 stress to hero who kills it. That hero gains buff: **"Residual Binding"** (+5% speed, +5% crit for rest of mission). +5 Arcane Swelling.

**C) "Harvest the binding magic."**
- Requirements: Pyromancer, Stormcaller, Cryomancer, or Alchemist in party
- Outcome: The caster carefully siphons the binding energy. The familiar shrieks and crumbles to dust.
- Mechanical effects: +15 stress to the caster. Caster gains buff: **"Siphoned Power"** (+15% skill damage for rest of mission). +10 Arcane Swelling.

**D) "Leave it."**
- Requirements: None
- Outcome: You walk past. Its three eyes follow you until you turn the corner.
- Mechanical effects: +5 stress to all heroes. No other effect.

### 15. The Hanging Cage

**Category:** Generic | **Rarity:** Rare

**Narrative setup:**
Suspended from the ceiling by rusted chains, a cage holds a figure so emaciated they seem more skeleton than person. But they breathe — a shallow, rattling whisper. Their eyes open as your torchlight reaches them, and they speak in a voice like tearing parchment: "I was put here as a warning. Do you know what I was warned about? I don't remember anymore. I only remember the dark." Whoever imprisoned them left a key on a hook, just out of their reach. Cruelty, not carelessness.

**Choices:**

**A) "Free them."**
- Requirements: None
- Outcome (Gamble):
  - 40% — Grateful survivor. They were a scholar. They share knowledge before departing: gain 1 rare trinket + 200 gold. -10 stress to all heroes.
  - 35% — They're too far gone. They collapse after being freed, murmuring thanks, and die within minutes. -5 stress to all heroes. No reward.
  - 25% — It's a trap. The "prisoner" is a mimic/shape-changer. Immediate combat encounter — **The Deception** (high damage, low HP, inflicts stress damage). +20 stress to all heroes (betrayal).

**B) "Interrogate them first."**
- Requirements: None
- Outcome: You question them through the bars. Their answers are rambling but contain fragments of truth.
- Mechanical effects: Scouting for 3 rooms. +10 stress to all heroes (the conversation is deeply disturbing). If the prisoner is genuine (75% chance), they tell you about a hidden room — gain access to one bonus loot room.

**C) "The Duelist cuts the chains."**
- Requirements: Duelist in party
- Outcome: A precise strike severs the chains without dropping the cage too hard. The prisoner is freed safely. The Duelist's efficiency impresses or terrifies them.
- Mechanical effects: Eliminates the 25% trap chance from choice A. Outcome is 55% grateful survivor / 45% dies peacefully. Same rewards as choice A for the survivor outcome. Duelist gains buff: **"Surgical Precision"** (+10% crit for rest of mission).

**D) "Walk away."**
- Requirements: None
- Outcome: The prisoner watches you leave. "I understand," they whisper. "I would have done the same."
- Mechanical effects: +15 stress to all heroes. No other effect.

**Stat-dependent modifier:** If any hero has stress > 100, they hear the prisoner differently — the prisoner seems to be speaking directly about that hero's fears. That hero gains +20 additional stress regardless of choice.

### FD-1. The Glacier's Heart

**Category:** Frozen Depths | **Rarity:** Uncommon

**Narrative setup:**
Deep within the ice wall, something glows — a crystalline formation pulsing with cold blue light, embedded like an organ in the glacier's body. The temperature drops sharply as you approach. Frost crawls across armor and skin. This is not natural cold; it's concentrated, intentional, alive. The ice around the crystal is perfectly clear, and through it you can see frozen shapes — creatures, objects, perhaps people — preserved for millennia.

**Choices:**

**A) "Extract the crystal."**
